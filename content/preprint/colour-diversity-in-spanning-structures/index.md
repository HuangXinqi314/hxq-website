abstract: >-
  We study colour diversity in spanning structures of properly edge-coloured graphs under Dirac-type minimum-degree assumptions. For every fixed $1/2<c\le 1$, every properly edge-coloured $n$-vertex graph with minimum degree at least $cn$ contains a Hamilton cycle using at least $cn-O(n^{1/2})$ distinct colours. We also prove that every subset of an $n\times n$ Latin square with at least $cn$ cells in every row and column contains a permutation using at least $cn-O(n^{2/3})$ distinct symbols. Both bounds are best possible up to the error term.
