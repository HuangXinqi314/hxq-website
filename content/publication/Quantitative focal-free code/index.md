abstract: Frameproof codes are a class of secure codes introduced by Boneh and Shaw in the context of digital fingerprinting, and have been widely studied from a combinatorial point of view. In this paper, we study a quantitative extension of frameproof codes and hypergraphs, referred to as {\it quantitative frameproof codes and hypergraphs}. We give asymptotically optimal bounds on the maximum sizes of these structures and determine their exact sizes for a broad range of parameters. In particular, we introduce a generalized version of the Erdős matching number in our proof and derive relevant estimates for it.
